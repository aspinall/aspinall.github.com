# Welcome to Craig Aspinall's Technical Blog

This is the first post on my new technical blog focused on building software products using AI tooling. 

## What to Expect

Over the coming weeks and months, I'll be sharing:

- **Real-world experiences** with AI development tools
- **Practical insights** from building products with AI assistance  
- **Honest evaluations** of tools, frameworks, and workflows
- **Strategic perspectives** on AI adoption in product development

## Why AI Tooling for Product Development?

The AI tooling landscape is evolving rapidly, but most content focuses on marketing hype rather than practical application. This blog aims to cut through the noise and provide actionable insights for developers and product teams.

## Coming Up

Future posts will cover topics like:

- Evaluating AI code assistants for different development workflows
- Building products with AI-powered features
- Managing the integration of AI tools into existing development processes
- The economics and productivity impacts of AI tooling adoption

Thanks for reading, and I look forward to sharing this journey with you!
