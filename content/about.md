# About Craig Aspinall

I'm a software developer and product builder with a focus on leveraging AI tooling to create better software products. This blog shares practical insights and real-world experiences from building products with AI assistance.

## Background

My experience spans software product development, where I've explored how AI tools can enhance development workflows, improve productivity, and enable new possibilities in product creation.

## Why This Blog?

The AI tooling landscape changes rapidly, with new development tools and workflows emerging constantly. Most content focuses on marketing claims or surface-level features, leaving practitioners without honest assessments of real-world effectiveness.

This blog aims to provide:

- **Practical insights** based on actual product development experience
- **Honest evaluations** of AI tools and their limitations
- **Strategic perspectives** on integrating AI into development workflows
- **Real-world case studies** from building products with AI assistance
