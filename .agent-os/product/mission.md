# Product Mission

## Pitch

Craig Aspinall's Technical Blog is a static site that helps software developers and product teams leverage AI tooling effectively by providing practical insights, experiences, and perspectives on building software products with AI assistance.

## Users

### Primary Customers

- Software developers: Looking to integrate AI tooling into their development workflows
- Product managers: Seeking to understand how AI tooling impacts product development
- Development teams: Wanting to improve productivity using AI-assisted development

### User Personas

**Senior Software Developer** (28-45 years old)
- **Role:** Technical Lead / Senior Engineer
- **Context:** Building software products, exploring AI-assisted development
- **Pain Points:** Staying current with AI development tools, finding practical implementation strategies
- **Goals:** Improve development efficiency, build better products with AI assistance, advance AI fluency

**Product Manager** (30-40 years old)
- **Role:** Product Manager / Technical PM
- **Context:** Managing software product development teams
- **Pain Points:** Understanding AI tooling capabilities, making informed decisions about AI adoption
- **Goals:** Deliver products faster, understand team productivity tools, make data-driven decisions

## The Problem

### Information Overload in AI Tooling

The AI tooling landscape changes rapidly with new development tools, code assistants, and workflows emerging constantly. Developers struggle to evaluate and adopt the right tools for their specific needs.

**Our Solution:** Curated, experience-based content focusing on practical AI tooling for product development.

### Gap Between AI Tool Marketing and Reality

Most AI tooling content focuses on marketing claims or surface-level features, leaving practitioners without honest assessments of real-world effectiveness and limitations.

**Our Solution:** Honest evaluations and insights based on actual product development experience with AI tools.

## Differentiators

### Experience-Based Content

Unlike marketing-focused AI tool content, we provide insights based on real product development experience with AI tooling. This results in practical, immediately applicable knowledge.

### Product Development Focus

Unlike generic AI productivity content, we focus specifically on how AI tooling integrates into software product development workflows. This results in more relevant and actionable insights.

## Key Features

### Core Features

- **Technical Blog Posts:** In-depth articles on AI development tools, workflows, and implementation strategies
- **Case Studies:** Real-world examples of AI tooling integration in product development
- **Experience Reports:** Personal insights from working with AI tools in production environments
- **Tool Reviews:** Practical evaluations of AI development tools and platforms

### Content Organization Features

- **Topic Categorization:** Content organized by development areas (coding, design, testing, deployment, product management)
- **AI Fluency Levels:** Content tagged for different levels of AI tool experience
- **Search Functionality:** Easy discovery of relevant content
- **RSS Feed:** Subscribe to new content updates
