# Product Mission (Lite)

Craig Aspinall's Technical Blog is a static site that helps software developers and product teams leverage AI tooling effectively by providing practical insights, experiences, and perspectives on building software products with AI assistance.

The blog serves development teams who need honest evaluations of AI development tools and workflows. Unlike marketing-focused AI tool content, it provides experience-based insights from real product development, focusing specifically on how AI tooling integrates into software development workflows.
